# how to play
simply run the code, you and the dealer draw cards, if you or the dealer at any point get more then 21 the game ends, with a loss of whoever crossed the score of 21

if someone gets 21 exactly the game ends with the winner being the one who gets exactly 21

if the score is lower then 21 the player has the option to draw another or stand, if he chooses to stand and dealer has less then 17, dealers draw cards until he has 17=> cards

after that if neither the dealer or the player have 21 or more, their scores are compared and the winner is decided on who has the bigger score
